How to use Celeste Tracker

Reading a row

Each row is a mod. The bar and x/y sides show how many sides (A, B, C and any extra sides) you've cleared. Hover over a bar to see the count for each side letter.

Slots

With All slots, each mod shows the save slot where you got furthest with it, and the Slot column says which. Hover over the number to see the other slots you played it in. Pick one slot in the Slot menu to see only that one.

The table

  • Click a row to open it: a mod opens to its level sets, a level set to its chapters, a chapter to its sides. Click the thin line down the left side to close it again.
  • Click a column name to sort by it; click it again to flip the order.
  • Columns shows or hides columns (Best and Berries are hidden at first).
  • Drag the line between two column names to resize them; double-click it to reset them.
  • The search matches mod names, authors, mod IDs, level sets, chapters and your notes.

Your ratings and notes

  • Rate how much you enjoyed a mod: hover over its row and click a star in the Rating column. Click the same star again to clear it.
  • Click ✎ Edit on a row (or open the mod) to write a note: where you stopped, what to try next. It saves as you type, and shows under the mod's name.
  • They're kept in the tracker's own file, never in your saves.

Staying up to date

The page updates by itself when the game saves (● live). Press Refresh after installing or updating mods, to read the Mods folder again.

The tracker only reads your save files and never changes them. Saves don't record dates, so it can't tell when you played something.
