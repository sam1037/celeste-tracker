A. The Filter panel open before anything is picked, and a hovered row

  1. 1 Filter takes the place of Show. Status, rating, difficulty, tags and notes are in one panel. Pick any number of choices: choices in one line widen the list (in progress or not started), and lines narrow it (and rated 4 or more). The numbers are how many mods each choice has.
  2. 2 Slot stays its own menu: it picks which slot's numbers you see, it doesn't hide mods.
  3. 3 Hovering a row shows ✎ Edit at the end of the name. It opens the mod at its Yours panel (rating, difficulty, tags, note). The faint stars also show, to rate it in one click.
